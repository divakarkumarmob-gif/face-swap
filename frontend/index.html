How to Call on WhatsApp with Live Face Swap

Use your swapped video stream seamlessly in WhatsApp Desktop, Web, Zoom & Google Meet

1

Start Live Swap in App

Select your desired face portrait on the left and click "Start Camera". Verify that your face is swapping in real time.

2

Enable Virtual Camera

Click the green "Start Virtual Camera" button. The app feeds real-time swapped frames into the virtual camera device.

3

Select Camera in WhatsApp

Open WhatsApp Desktop (or Web). Go to Settings > Audio & Video > Camera, and select "OBS Virtual Camera".

4

Start Your Video Call!

Call anyone on WhatsApp! They will see your AI face-swapped video stream in crystal-clear real time.

Pro-Tip: If Virtual Camera is not installed yet, click "Popout Clean Window" and share that window via WhatsApp screen share or OBS Studio!
